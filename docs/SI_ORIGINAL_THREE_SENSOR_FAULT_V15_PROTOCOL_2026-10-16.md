## Research question
v14's independent anchor detects reference-sensor disagreement, but treats anchor failure as reference failure. v15 tests whether probabilistic **per-sensor** reliability and a conservative reference-repair guard protect original SI neural decisions compared with a nominally matched Transformer, smaller GRU, classical majority vote and Bayesian fault filtering. These are **hand-specified models**, not learned discovery of general fault physics or SI.

Candidate states enumerate 5 target-polarity histories with priors (.65,.08,.08,.08,.11), 4 reference states (healthy, early reversal, late reversal, late high-variance noise) with priors (.76,.12,.08,.04), 3 anchor states (healthy, early reversal, late reversal) with priors (.83,.12,.05), and 2 current target polarities. **120 exact finite joint hypotheses**, independent hand-chosen class priors, Gaussian sensor likelihoods, log-space marginalization of theta_i. Return P(current z+), P(reference fault), P(anchor fault), entropy using ONLY observed x/y/a and known strengths. No model reads private z/theta, hidden family labels, query truth, purchased future signals or unpurchased probe.

Classical control: cheap last-three redundant-sensor majority/correlation estimate of target polarity, and hand-specified full fault Bayesian posterior + cost-sensitive binary action/probe selection. Both classical anchor-assisted strategies pay fixed synthetic third-sensor fee **0.025 reward**. Their likelihood priors are privileged simulator knowledge and cannot be called a fair equal-knowledge learned baseline.

Neural: train **unchanged canonical v12** original SI hybrid 64,614 params, Transformer 64,637, small GRU 10,283 (not size-matched) in raw and belief modes; 180 actor updates+200 chosen-arm critic-only steps, batch96, alternating stationary/one target flip; 2 CPU threads, fixed original optimizer settings. No model receives fault labels. Guarded repair uses belief model and substitutes scaled observed anchor into reference **only if P(reference_bad)>.55 and P(reference_bad)-P(anchor_bad)>.20**. It pays third-sensor fee 0.025 regardless of whether replacement occurs; no hard-coded true fault or family label can select the route. Compare **no-fee raw**, **no-fee belief**, **fee-charged always substitute**, **fee-charged v14 consistency repair**, guarded repair, classical vote and Bayesian filter on identical worlds. Training two neural variants and calculating the filter consumes extra resources, not equivalent active FLOPs.

Nine fixed evaluation families: base, one target flip, two target flips, late reference reversal, early reference reversal, late high-noise reference dropout, anchor reversal, simultaneous late reference+anchor reversal, simultaneous target+anchor failure. Simultaneous fault hypotheses receive very low product prior, a hard negative for overconfidence and transfer.

## Frozen go/no-go
**A Fault-model/majority control:** Bayesian charged reward beats cheap charged majority by >=0.015 on BOTH late reference and anchor reversals, loses <=0.015 on stationary, and is >=0.015 above the unpriced raw hybrid control for BOTH reversals. Correct fault posterior orders P(reference_bad)>P(anchor_bad) on >=65% of actual late-reference episodes and conversely on >=65% of actual anchor-reversal episodes. Do NOT claim simultaneous fault isolation without explicitly measuring posterior mass on both-fault hypotheses.
**B Guarded neural repair:** at least one FULL backbone must beat v14 same-fee consistency repair by >=0.08 on anchor reversal AND >=0.015 on reference reversal, lose no more than 0.015 versus v14 same-fee consistency repair on base; reach >= unpriced raw minus 0.02 on BOTH reference and anchor reversals AND >= unpriced raw minus 0.03 on BOTH simultaneous-fault families; win >=2/3 paired seeds for all improvements. Any failed subcondition means NO-GO.
**C Original-hybrid advantage:** same guarded method improves over matched Transformer by >=0.015 net reward in stationary/reference/anchor worlds, wins >=2/3 paired seeds each, and has <=1.5x Transformer training CPU cost. No active-FLOP/efficiency conclusion without instrumentation.
**D Novelty/transfer:** full hypothesis set and fault priors are pre-specified rather than learned. A narrow positive is not generalized unknown-model discovery or SI breakthrough; any gate pass only authorizes further CPU work, never GPU scaling, main merge or background automation. Preserve negative results and exact-head CI.
