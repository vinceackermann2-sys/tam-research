## Actual additive change

- `tam_research/si_original_factual_replay_v3.py`: `SIOriginalFactualReplayV3` keeps bounded **raw public sensory before/after histories**, accepted only **after** the parent validates a chosen executed 4-action motor command, finite scalar actual reward, one appended public next observation, unchanged prior public prefix and caller-attested provenance. It never accepts a hidden world state, imagined rollout or reward for an unchosen action.
- `train_sensor_world_replay(optimizer,world,count)` selects only that world's actual, previously received public experiences and re-encodes the whole **before** history with gradients through the five distinct native sensor encoders, the exact original alternating attention / state-space / sparse-expert backbone, recurrent ACT-style reasoning, the same action-conditioned latent world transition used by planning, reward/value heads, and the verifier and halt heads.
- The **observed after** state is re-encoded strictly inside `torch.no_grad()`. This is a self-bootstrapped latent-space representation target for actual next public observation, NOT a privileged latent teacher, true simulator state or pretrained semantic embedding. Its loss can have representational collapse; stop-gradient alone is not an adequate anti-collapse guarantee.
- Prediction targets are the **actual** next public sensory history and **chosen action's actual observed reward**. The value head receives **one-step factual reward only**, NOT an invented optimal-Q, MCTS backup or long-horizon return. Halting receives a detached, clearly heuristic observed prediction-error threshold; verifier receives clipped observed single-step representation error. Sparsity balance is retained.
- No action CE/ imitation from historical executed actions: a logged executed action is **not** evidence that it was the best action. The direct policy action head is explicitly left untrained, and discrete argmax planning/search remains not end-to-end policy-trained.
- Tests prove nonzero gradients reach five encoders, core original blocks and recurrent latent state, direct policy head untouched; disjoint next-only text-token embedding has zero gradient; invalid unexecuted feedback rejects; cloned immutable raw records are world-filtered, RAM-only and bounded.

## Honest limitations, risks and next gate

1. The feedback provenance string is still supplied by the caller: **not authenticated or externally independently verified**. A safe adapter with non-executable hidden state and trusted transition receipts is required before sealed evaluation.
2. V3 does **not** optimize direct action selection or differentiable selection through search. It is world-model/reward/verifier/halting *auxiliary* learning through real sensory data, not a complete trained agent or policy. Expert specialization, compute-saving ACT benefits and hallucination safety remain unproven.
3. Differentiable latent transition training is self-referential and may collapse. Learned encoder coordinates can drift while older V1/V2 memory keys remain cached. **Memory re-encoding/reindexing and a target-network or stabilizing contrastive term need an explicit test** before scaling. Raw sensory replay is RAM-only and not included in V2 persistence.
4. Early V1 world-scoped fast weights remain active at inference, but replay gradient does not train the fast-weight update optimizer itself; world-scope prevents fast-adapter contamination, not shared-backbone forgetting. World-filtered replay with a shared backbone may still transfer knowledge/forget.
5. The V3 test is an engineering fixture, **not an environment-specific performance benchmark**. The prior 64,614-param v26 and PCMA negative findings remain valid for their particular proxy implementations. They do not evaluate the user's complete architecture.
