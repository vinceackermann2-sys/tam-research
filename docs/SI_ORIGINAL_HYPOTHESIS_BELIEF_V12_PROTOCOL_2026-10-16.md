## Purpose and negative prior results
V11 confirmed paired normal-task gains for v10 contextual logged-value training over v9, but severe hidden-sensor-drift failures (v10 hybrid drift reward 0.7414, Transformer 0.6826, known-change-family Bayes 0.8461). The original SI hybrid also lost to matched Transformer on familiar worlds. The next falsifiable question: does explicit **causal, probabilistic sensor-polarity/change-point inference**, injected identically into competing neural controllers, improve cost-aware information purchase when observation rules shift?

This is a **hand-specified hypothesis filter**, not autonomously discovered/learned world dynamics. It knows a candidate family of zero or one calibration sensor flip at index 2,3,4, but **does not see actual flip time, world family, private hidden polarity z, or future probe observation**. Calibration data are six noisy target/reference pairs, known strengths and probe price; final action and correctness occur after decision. No private simulator labels enter neural training.

- Likelihood under candidate hypothesis z∈{+1,-1} and one of {stationary, flipped-before-calibration-time2, time3, time4}. For each calibration pair integrate unknown true mode theta_i analytically as mixture of two Gaussians; multiply across six observations. Prior P(stationary)=0.5, P(one flip)=0.5 divided equally across 3 change-points. Marginalize all hypotheses to compute **P(z=+ | prior observations)** and **P(any change | prior observations)**. Never route using actual scenario label/time; test that secret metadata/hidden query truth and unbought y_probe cannot change these posteriors. This is a *model-based strong privileged-prior reference*, not fair general learned adaptation.
- Feature injection: same 7 scalar channels as frozen v8 protocol. At query and after-probe positions only, replace two otherwise-zero slots with bounded centered polarity posterior `2P(z+)-1` and change probability. **No new trainable parameters or additional observed sensors**. Calibration tokens unchanged, both original SI hybrid and Transformer receive exactly the same engineered belief; raw-input ablations receive zero slots. A smaller GRU raw/belief pair tests whether the feature benefit is specific to the hybrid. Original hybrid 64,614 params vs Transformer 64,637, GRU 10,283 (not parameter matched). The original's dormant persistent episodic memory/fast adapter are not activated by this test.
- All six models trained with the **same v10-style frozen logged-bandit update algorithm** and the same optimizer/data streams, only feature slots differ: train binary final action from observed executed action + binary correctness under randomized buy/skip; freeze backbone; train two contextual expected-final-correctness Q values from **only the randomly observed** buy/skip arm, then choose buy iff `Q_buy - Q_skip > known_cost`. The optimizer never accesses hidden z, hidden change-point, analytic Bayes purchase label, or unpurchased probe.
- Training distribution: equal stream of unseen **stationary** and **one-flip** episodes (alternating deterministic minibatch families), known filter prior 0.5, no direct family label. Evaluation worlds shared across models: **base stationary**, **one_flip** with concealed time in {2,3,4} (both training families, heldout episodes); **early_flip** with time∈{1,5} (unseen); **double_flip** with two hidden changes during calibration (unseen incompatible with one-flip hypothesis); and **weak_calibration** (OOD sensor-quality family).
- Controls: causal mixture Bayesian decision using inferred p(z), stationary-misspecified Bayesian, classical always/never buy with frozen stationary Bayesian inference. Bayesian knows likelihoods and hypothesis family, so it is a **reference with external structure**, not an equal-prior architecture comparison. In particular double_flip violates its model; report poor transfer rather than selectively ignoring it.

## Predeclared go/no-go gates
1. **Mechanism usefulness:** At least one of full hybrid/Transformer belief features must improve mean one-flip reward by >=0.025 over its *own same-seed raw control*, win >=2/3 paired seeds, and not reduce base reward by >0.015. At least 2/3 paired OOD environments (early_flip, double_flip, weak_calibration) must not deteriorate by >0.02. Keep probes interior [0.1,0.9], conditional value Brier <=0.16 (base and one-flip). Otherwise NO-GO this fixed Bayesian feature method.
2. **Original SI architectural advantage (separate):** hybrid+belief must exceed matched Transformer+belief on one-flip and base mean reward by >=0.015 each and >=2/3 paired seed wins on both; no worse on both early_flip/double_flip, and <=1.5× Transformer actual CPU train time. Absent this, do NOT claim original architecture advantage even if common filter improves both.
3. **True new-law transfer:** It may be that no-specified-hypothesis posterior is valid for one-flip but confidently wrong for double_flip. If learned+Bayes controls collapse under unseen double flip, record that as OOD model-class misspecification; cannot call it online generalized change discovery.
