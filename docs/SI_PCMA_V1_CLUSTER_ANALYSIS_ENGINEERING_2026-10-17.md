## New evidence-quality feature

`tam_research/si_pcma_v1_cluster_analysis.py` is a **pure** analysis module for the already-developed uniform-first-arm randomized factual logger (PR #1505). No environment is constructed, no model is trained and no heldout-world result is produced.

Each input row records a factual two-action pair, its world/session/pair grouping, pre-action public observation, chosen first arm, known probability 0.25, one frozen public continuation policy and both actually observed net rewards.

- **Primary descriptive estimand:** mean of per-world Horvitz–Thompson contrasts: probe arm 3 versus a predeclared **uniform mixture** of guess arms 0/1/2. The mixture is not a post-hoc “best guess” chosen after viewing test returns and is NOT the full strongest adaptive no-probe policy.
- **World-level aggregation:** equal weight for independent worlds even when one has a longer horizon. Within each world, every actual pair contributes to its world average; pairs are not spuriously treated as independent replications. For world `w`, action `a` score is `sum(I(A=a) × Y / 0.25) / number_of_pairs_in_world`.
- **Reported uncertainty:** sample standard deviation of per-world arm differences and standard error `SD_world / sqrt(number_of_worlds)`. A world-level SE is only a diagnostic; it is **NOT** a registered confidence interval, independent hypothesis test or correctness certificate.
- **Coverage diagnostics:** total opportunities, number of independent worlds, factual observations in each arm, worlds where probe was actually observed, and worlds with all three guessing actions. Missing arms remain unobserved and are NOT filled with hypothetical rewards.
- **Fails closed** on repeated world/session/pair keys, wrong assignment propensities, altered factual action/fees/rewards, nonconsecutive transitions, mixed continuation policies, invalid continuation actions and fewer than two independent worlds. No automatic retry or alternate seed substitution.

## What the effect does and does NOT mean

The randomized first action with known propensity can identify an **assignment effect** on realized two-action return under the *same frozen downstream continuation* and the sampled behavior-policy state distribution. A probe's immediate fee and lost guessing opportunity both count. Since world dynamics depend on prior assigned actions, the observed state distribution is generated by this very randomized behavior policy. Results are not automatically transportable to another policy, longer contexts, new worlds or tool tasks.

World clusters, not sequential pairs, are the natural uncertainty units. Even clustered standard errors do not correct for overfitting from choosing the world generator, continuation, metric, model, comparison or stopping time after seeing results. The unit fixtures in CI are **not scientific samples**.

The development-only frozen continuation in #1505 may be biased towards some mapping classes. Even a positive probe effect on this controlled task would not establish:
- That trained PCMA chooses probes optimally or robustly.
- That the original SI hybrid beats a compute-accounted matched Transformer, higher-compute Transformer, GRU or best cheap factual memory/change detector.
- That actual causal memory/online weight adaptation is necessary, rather than lookup/TD/known-laws.
- General intelligence, self-improvement or a breakthrough.
