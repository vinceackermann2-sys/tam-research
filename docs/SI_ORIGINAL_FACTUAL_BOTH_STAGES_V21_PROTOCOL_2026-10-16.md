## Frozen causal training method
Each fresh seed:
1. Train **ONE** unchanged v19 disjoint-Q actor/critic per architecture with **160 full-backbone actor +240 head-only critic AdamW steps**, batch96, 2 CPU threads; private latent outcome never enters policy input. Independently clone identical actor for frozen v19 and v20 Q0-only actual-return control.
2. Frozen v20 control: run unchanged `v20.refit_q0` with **160 first-Q-only AdamW steps** on logged actual net outcomes.
3. New `FactoredPolicy`: freeze the identical canonical v19 model, copy its preexisting v19 second-Q head initial weights into two 3→2 **correctness logits heads** and its Q0 weights into a separate Q0 head. Train **160 additional Q1-head-only steps**, independent randomized first low/high purchased, independent randomized second buy/skip, executed frozen terminal classifier, actual **observed correctness 0/1** as target. Two heads train on DISJOINT even/odd logged trajectories and ONLY the actually bought/skipped second arm. Never train on unpurchased signals, simulator answer labels, or Bayesian optimal purchase labels. Deploy Q1 as `0.5*(sigmoid(Q1A)+sigmoid(Q1B))` and then **subtract public cost of second purchase exactly**, outside the neural predictor. No second purchase means no second fee.
4. Freeze everything except the new 3-output Q0 head. Fit it for **160 further steps** to ACTUALLY observed full-trajectory reward under a frozen Q1-greedy continuation. Randomize first stop/cheap/strong and randomize the second purchase. For bought-first episodes accept only logs whose second randomized arm equals the greedy Q1 arm (known 50% propensity independent of latent correctness), then observe actual correctness minus second fee. Regress Q0 on this **continuation reward**; deduct the public first fee separately in the Q0 decision rule. Stop episodes use actually executed stop correctness. No oracle/Bayes policy label or unobserved counterfactual outcome in training. The new heads see the old frozen backbone's 3-dimensional output, and might be bottlenecked by that representation.
5. **Classical cheap comparator**: an entirely separate deterministic **contextual ridge** value policy trained from actual randomized logged outcomes in **6000 base worlds per seed**, with engineered public-sensor features and explicit cost subtraction. It uses the known Gaussian signed likelihood ratio to make terminal binary actions (privileged structural prior), so it is NOT equal-information or equal-compute compared to learned neural terminal classifier; however it is a strong test of whether complex backbones are necessary on this toy task. Do not credit a pure reward gain to architecture if cheap comparator also succeeds.

All policies evaluated on EXACT same v17/v19 causal paired opposite-label worlds and public prices. Family `base`; OOD `high_cost_ood`, `weak_sensors_ood`, `strong_discount_ood`. The last changes only announced prices, such that strong-probe selection should increase. Rewards are REAL correctness minus actual purchased first/second costs. Keep never, cheap only, strong only, always both, greedy and analytic two-stage known-law Bayes as evaluation-only controls. Base first/second purchase rates, realized cost and heldout randomized **observed-arm** second correctness MSE are mandatory, not just raw reward.
