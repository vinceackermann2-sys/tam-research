## Fully specified minimal POMDP
A hidden binary world mode theta is +/-1, sampled equiprobably. The agent sees an initial noisy cue x0 ~ Normal(theta*mu0,1), sensor quality mu0~Uniform(0.12,1.50), optional probe quality muP~Uniform(0.8,2.1) and cost c~Uniform(0.04,0.31), but does not see theta. It decides to **probe** (pay c; see independent xP~Normal(theta*muP,1)) or skip, then selects final action a in {-1,+1}. Reward = 1[a==theta] - c*1[probe]. A no-probe guess, always probe, random guess, and **analytically optimal Bayes value-of-information rule** provide strong controls. Under the 20% initial-cue sign-flip withheld simulator, use (a) a correctly specified mixture-likelihood Bayesian upper reference and (b) the Gaussian Bayesian strategy with misspecified cue model. A second holdout increases probe costs to Uniform(0.04,0.49). The neural models see neither hidden theta nor unpurchased probe xP at decision time.

In the Gaussian case log prior odds=2*mu0*x0. Probe changes odds by 2*muP*xP. Closed-form expected success if probing is p*Phi(muP+log_odds/(2*muP))+(1-p)*Phi(muP-log_odds/(2*muP)); probe only when expected gain exceeds c. Under sign-flipped initial cues, posterior prior likelihood is correct 80/20 Gaussian mixture, then identical probe update. The exact Bayes controller is optimal **within this tiny specified binary-world model only**.

## Models / training
- Compact **DenseControl**: stage0 MLP predicts buy-probe decision from (x0,mu0,muP,c); stage1 MLP selects action from (same, observed xP if purchased, purchased flag).
- Compact **RecurrentControl**: same information represented as two causal tokens and processed through GRU, separate probe/action output heads. Stage0 prefix includes no probe outcome even when stage1 uses it. These are task learnability controls; they are **not** the original SI/Transformer model, have unmatched parameter counts, and use supervised Gaussian Bayes-probe decisions plus hidden-mode action targets during synthetic training. No real-world training.
- AdamW 240 optimizer steps, batch256, LR .002, wd .01; CPU two threads. Train on Gaussian only. For action-label training half the samples reveal the optional probe, half do not, with randomized training-time mask. Evaluation chooses its own probe action and sees only purchased probe. Score realized reward, final accuracy, paid probe rate and CPU training seconds. No interaction loop trained via RL.

### Task-validity gate
Task valid for future learned SI comparisons if Gaussian Bayes beats BOTH never-probe and always-probe by >=.03 mean realized reward in at least 2/3 paired seeds; optimal controller probes in (0.05,0.95) fraction of worlds; out-of-distribution corruption imposes measurable cost on Gaussian-misspecified Bayes versus correctly specified Bayes; and the benchmark passes causal tests. If task fails, redesign. A learned policy may or may not match the Bayes reference; report failures. **No authority to scale GPU or claim original SI superiority**.
