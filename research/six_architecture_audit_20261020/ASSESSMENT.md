# Six architecture research assessment — 20 October 2026

**Conclusion: no demonstrated breakthrough among the six.** Fifty recorded training runs and the source audit support selective continuation and specific redesigns. Conv7 is the strongest new continuation candidate. These finite development studies do not justify declaring every unvalidated family a failure or claiming a completed100M/2B confirmation. Final tables are generated from immutable attempt records; the interrupted original GPU extension remains explicitly unverified.

## Scope and evidence

The audit covers `main`, the canonical CPW branch, the bounded RLT/AX branch and the user's exact SI Original world-scoped V2 branch, plus relevant GitHub issue callbacks. `SOURCE_INVENTORY.json` indexes698 research files with content hashes; indexing is not a claim that every historical experiment was independently rerun. Historical callbacks are evidence reports, not an independent reproduction of their remote checkpoints. New experiments are labeled development, capacity, or calibration rather than confirmatory science.

The user's ceilings are strict: every new trained model <=100,000,000 actual parameters, every model <=2,000,000,000 training exposures. Historical “100M” models had about 101.8M parameters, and the attention8 parent ran2,000,027,648 exposures. Those historical configurations exceed the current request and were not reused as eligible new runs. A width 504/24-layer Conv7 family now fits: Transformer 99,051,624; attention8 parent99,059,688; Conv7 99,043,576. These near100M configurations were **counted on the meta device, not trained**.

## CPW — continue the efficient local mixer; prove binding separately

**Best historical evidence:** the sequence-only successor beat Transformer mean NLL 6.526272 with 6.414157 over three fresh 25M-token pairs, won3/3, and reported1.0643× training throughput at fewer parameters. This is the strongest practical continuation signal in this six-track audit, though it is a short training horizon and a different parameter count. [Original callback #1254](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/issues/1254).

**Mechanistic obstacle:** fifteen one-token shift/pointwise blocks have a maximum15-token receptive field. This follows directly from the dependency graph. More optimization or a bigger vocabulary cannot let that unchanged model bind a query to a value256 tokens away. It can still be useful as a local language mixer. Sparse WORLD additions did not outperform sequence-only convincingly; the original late AFM uses additive positive-feature memory without an explicit targeted erase mechanism, and initialization probes found substantial key overlap/filler writes. Early AFM strengthens distant gradients, but gradient magnitude is not learned query-specific routing.

The old v4/v5 recall task had a perfect key-blind positional shortcut, so the historical Transformer 100% score does not validate key binding. The corrected task instead holds the prefix fixed and changes only the query key. [Benchmark audit and correction](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/issues/1307), [four-query diagnostic](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/issues/1452).

The vectorized AFM training path materializes cumulative rank-by-rank matrices across the sequence (`[batch,time,rank,rank]`). A bounded inference-state design does not imply bounded training activation memory. Include this cost in any efficiency claim, alongside readout capacity and selective addressing.

**New first batch:** at 500 updates/2.56M input exposures, the compact-vocabulary Transformer, sequence-only, late AFM and early AFM all remained around chance and answered zero complete four-query groups. The positive control failed. This cannot fairly kill AFM or validate any memory architecture. A separately frozen5000-update calibration uses new source/initialization/data and only trains candidates if the Transformer first learns. See `EXTENSION_PROTOCOL.md`.

**Decision:** continue the simpler local mixer as an efficiency hypothesis. Hold long-memory superiority claims. If the calibrated corrected task is learnable for full/R1 attention but not AFM, stop scaling that AFM implementation and investigate competitive addressing, selective writes and delta/erase updates. A hybrid that adds ordinary attention is a useful control, not automatically a new breakthrough.

**Completed recovery calibration:** after the original extension became unavailable, a separately declared fresh Transformer control trained 51,200,000 exposures on the current A10080GB. Accuracy was1.37%,3.32%,2.15%,4.10% at delays32/64/128/256, with 0/128 complete four-query groups. Its checkpoint is saved locally. The control gate failed, so all conditional AFM/sequence/R1 comparisons were skipped. This diagnoses the current task/training recipe, not an AFM-versus-learned-attention result. The lost original extension remains an explicit unknown outcome.

## PGW — stop promoting the old mechanism; new V3 needs a learnable fair control

**Historical result:** the three-seed core attribution panel found no supported predictor/cross-chunk carry mechanism. Carry NLL 7.160164 versus reset7.159495 means retaining state did not help. The small gain against Transformer was also present in simpler local-width controls. [Authoritative #1201/#1213 callback](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/issues/1201).

**Current V3 obstacle:** structural paths, gradients and a sealed corpus are implementation prerequisites, not learned utility. Top-k event selection has a surrogate gradient; four slots and continual distractor writes create retention pressure. The native auxiliary prediction losses of PGW and full attention have different target representations/history access, and near-equal instantiated counts do not establish active-capacity or compute parity. These are unresolved attribution confounds.

**New result:** across three initializations, all three arms—hybrid, position-fair full attention and chunk-only—answered every new development case “not found”:50% overall,0% present retrieval,100% missing. Their loss is near the label-prior solution. Thus even the positive control did not learn retrieval at 256 updates. Aggregate accuracy alone would disguise complete retrieval failure. This first screen is **inconclusive for V3 efficacy**, not evidence that a workspace is mathematically impossible.

**Deeper-control continuation:** three fresh 176,801-parameter, three-layer Transformer controls then trained 5,000 updates each (10,879,680 exposures), answer CE alone, on new generated data. All three still had0% present-answer accuracy at every delay; missing rejection was96.67–100%. Every fixed-prefix eight-query group failed. The predeclared control gate failed, so no conditional native-PGW runs were started. The deeper control is not parameter/compute-matched to PGW. Posthoc CE decomposition on the same consumed development panel found roughly50% missing probability for both present and absent inputs; lower loss partly reflects plausible payload probabilities, not successful query-addressed retrieval. No checkpoint or threshold was selected from this analysis. This remains a concrete learnability/control blocker, not a fair architecture falsification.

**Decision:** no broad PGW breakthrough and no reason to scale the old unsupported design. Before more V3 language training, demonstrate controlled learnability, split presence/payload diagnostics, match auxiliary information/supervision, establish gradient-active capacity, and measure full forward/backward cost. An independently valid baseline must solve the task first.

## Conv7 — strongest new positive signal, with a known extrapolation hazard

The historical attention8 parent trained about 2B tokens but lost to Transformer by+.045087 NLL while gaining7.50% throughput, missing its+.015 quality ceiling. That is a real negative for that specific sparse-attention schedule. Conv7 adds causal depthwise token mixing in the sixteen otherwise tokenwise blocks. [Frozen postmortem](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/blob/4362402592ac0cad5ed3d12f1d7defe68739a2b5/research/reduced_attention/attention8_pair1_successor_postmortem_v1.md).

**New measured pilot:**11.295M-class models, three seeds,1,048,576 GPT2 training exposures each, WikiText2, context 128, same A10040GB and optimizer recipe. Mean validation NLL: Transformer 6.433108; parent6.395772; Conv7 6.362698. Conv7 beat both controls in all three seeds and averaged~52,835 tokens/s versus~46,972 for Transformer (~1.125×), while the parent was faster still (~70,991). Count difference Conv7 versus Transformer was16 parameters. The convolution is strictly causal and its zero-initialized gate wakes convolution-weight gradients on the second update.

**Limits:** this is a small, source-authored width-generalized implementation and a very short training horizon on a small second corpus. The depth/schedule, convolution residual ordering, default convolution initialization and attention primitive follow the prototype. The new models use8 heads rather than the frozen512-wide prototype's16, and their construction/RNG order differs. They are new configurations, not exact reproductions of the old100M model. No large-model inference, long-context capability, equal-compute frontier, or 2B outcome follows. The parent also looked good at short horizons historically. A fresh 20M-class/25M-token FineWeb development screen is frozen separately before launch; only a later replicated long-horizon study could settle durability.

**Completed longer FineWeb screen:** one fresh seed, approximately20.5M parameters,24,997,888 exposures/model, context 256, all three arms on the same A10080GB. Transformer NLL 5.742762; attention8 parent5.801374; Conv7 5.706767. Conv7 improved NLL by 0.035995 against Transformer and 0.094607 against its parent, while delivering95,699 versus86,932 training tokens/s (1.1008×). All frozen development gates passed. The parameter-count difference from Transformer was only 3,056. The same Conv7 checkpoint exactly replayed its recorded NLL; zeroing its16 convolution gains increased NLL to 6.342701 (+0.635934). This demonstrates reliance on the trained branch, not clean training attribution or independent confirmation. All new checkpoint/data hashes are retained locally.

**Decision:** continue Conv7 first. Replicate the longer result with independent seeds, then evaluate longer horizons and a counted near99M configuration within the user's ceiling. Include stronger recurrent/hybrid baselines and actual inference/kernel cost. The positive second-corpus result is development evidence, not a breakthrough declaration; width/context/corpus/LR differ from the small pilot, so cross-study changes cannot be attributed to training horizon alone.

## CHM — V4 can memorize; generalization and useful indexed retrieval remain open

Historical CHM-v0 lost rare-fact addressability through lossy page routing. CHM-v1's exact index preserved nearest-neighbor answers on a synthetic representation task, but fewer counted vector reads are not measured GPU speed. The classical index is not itself a novel learned architecture. The100M DAEC Stage-C attempt stopped with missing trained hard/flat evaluator parity and no aggregate retrieval gain; those negative records remain unchanged. [CHM-v1 mechanism](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/blob/4362402592ac0cad5ed3d12f1d7defe68739a2b5/architectures/chm_v1/README.md), [Stage-C #1323](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/issues/1323).

**Current obstacles:** source address, literal payload copy and abstention are different failure modes. Older soft attention scores were not actual hard memory reads. V4 hard-copies the final chosen CODE, but the second hop still conditions on a soft mixture of first-hop embeddings. Gold TRAIN address supervision also makes an ordinary decoder an unfair comparison unless that supervision budget is matched.

**New measured capacity result:** all three fresh 274,187-parameter V4 initializations memorized24/24 positive TRAIN rotations and rejected the one TRAIN no-match case after 256 updates/18,912 exposures. This rules out inability to fit that tiny TRAIN set at the tested recipe. It establishes **no held-out benefit**. Position/template memorization is still possible; original reserved DEVELOPMENT/TEST entities were not opened by this batch.

**New out-of-sample entity diagnostic:** frozen checkpoints were then evaluated, without training, on all 63 remaining native TRAIN entities (eight rotations per positive family and one negative/entity). Across seeds, direct answer accuracy ranged0.79–16.07%, overwrite10.12–21.23%, two-hop6.55–13.49%; no-match rejection ranged4.76–82.54%. A single entity microfit has not learned a robust reusable binding/abstention rule. This is an exploratory optimizer-unseen panel inside the TRAIN namespace, not the reserved independent DEVELOPMENT/TEST study. No matched trained baseline was run, so no superiority or family-wide impossibility follows. Per-entity clusters and checkpoint hashes are retained in `results/chm_unseen`.

**Decision:** continue a cheap generalization test rather than another100M run. Use multiple independent entity clusters, unseen names/positions, strict query swaps, separate source/copy/gate metrics, same-supervision Transformer and exact index-versus-flat checkpoint parity. With32 independent negative entities, even zero false positives cannot establish FPR<5% at 95% confidence; prospectively size a new cohort (at least59 error-free negatives without multiplicity correction). [Prospective statistics](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/blob/4362402592ac0cad5ed3d12f1d7defe68739a2b5/research/chm_v4_grouped_go_no_go_1450.md).

**Broad training / first reserved DEVELOPMENT result:** three new 274,187-parameter pointers trained 4,096 updates each across all 64 TRAIN entities with balanced families and the same gold-source supervision. TRAIN positive-family accuracy varied35–92%; DEVELOPMENT remained poor: direct3.1–19.5%, overwrite16.4–21.9%, two-hop5.5–14.1%, no-match rejection6.25–56.25%. All three preliminary gates failed. Thus additional entity diversity did not fix the present recipe; both optimization stability and unseen-identity/address/copy/gate generalization remain blockers. In one model, DEVELOPMENT two-hop first-source accuracy reached75% while final answers were14.1%, isolating a downstream addressing/copy problem. This is a flat Transformer-based pointer study, not a hierarchical-index acceleration result. The new DEVELOPMENT panel is now consumed for this study; native TEST remains closed.

## RLT — retain the state mechanism, repair robustness; distinguish the published model

The repository's original clean-room serial RLT and later light-state/associative-scan/bounded-chunk successors are different architectures. Negative evidence for a modified parallel recurrence cannot be relabeled a falsification of the original feedback decoder.

**New fidelity finding:** the current paper's equations9–11 specify an encoder residual plus gated, scaled normalized decoder feedback. The older repository prototype still says merge is unspecified and uses a concatenation projection, then an extra merge normalization. Its tied blocks and layerwise memory projections also differ from the paper's reported untied experimental layouts/shared memory group. `rlt_paper_merge.py` implements and derivative-tests the published merge alone; it is not a full reproduced RLT. This source discrepancy should be resolved before another expensive claim about the original model.

Historical15.1M residual RLT lost to Transformer by+.031907 NLL at equal A100 training time, with 91% throughput. Adaptive mixing also failed its language gate. The latest fixed-update bounded-state panel passed only 1/3 initializations. Conversely, a successful same-weight state-erasure experiment showed that an individual trained model really used its carried state. [RLT evidence branch](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/tree/6dd09e019a65ea307f9027f6aabd4f31b11a5b3c/experiments/rlt).

**New study:** fresh bounded carry/reset/gate-bias4 controls, identical counts and update/data budgets, three predeclared initializations. All three carry runs are now complete: two passed the per-class retention gate, while the third fell to 50% at gap 127. Two successes demonstrate learnable state use; the failed third confirms unresolved initialization sensitivity. This is one synthetic task, not language superiority. An analytic concern is multiplicative state decay; a larger initial gate bias is a parameter-neutral, predeclared intervention, not guaranteed improvement. The generated table retains every reset/bias result and any incomplete attempt. A separately frozen inference-only length/erasure diagnostic tests what transfers beyond the128-token training maximum.

**Completed robustness findings:** all three bias4 interventions failed the declared retention gate; all three reset controls gave50% at long gaps. On the new length 256/512/1024 paired-prefix diagnostic, one carry checkpoint (seed 202610202) retained both classes perfectly through gap 1023 and fell to 50% with the same weights' carry erased. The other two carry checkpoints and all three bias4 checkpoints scored50% at these longer gaps. This isolates real recurrent memory in one trained model and substantial seed/length fragility in the recipe. It does not support broad robustness or language scaling.

The newly published [RLT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07591) specifically reports algorithmic state tracking and feedback ablations; it also reports that chunking affects different tasks differently. That supports testing feedback-dependent tasks and preserving architectural fidelity. It does not establish that this repository's modified variant wins natural language modeling.

**Decision:** do not scale the current bounded variant for general language on existing evidence. Continue bounded robustness/causal-state diagnostics, and independently implement/evaluate the actual published recurrence on state tracking before judging that separate architecture. Preserve the distinction between state usefulness, length generalization, language quality and training cost.

## SI Original V2 — repair the training graph before scaling the whole system

The user's selected branch implements actual hybrid blocks, sparse experts, modality encoders, halting, search, factual memory, fast adaptation and replay interfaces. It fixes cross-world fast-weight leakage. Older64,614-parameter proxy/PCMA NO-GO results do not settle the full original proposal. [Exact selected V2](https://github.com/vinceackermann2-sys/tam-research/tree/9298c117e736676269107374a402519e45e52f71).

**New concrete blocker:** `_search` consumes `value_head`, but search is under `no_grad`; policy CE trains direct logits, and native replay loss trains dynamics/reward/verifier/halting without value loss. Thus the planning value head is untrained. Measured gradient coverage confirmed no value-head training in all six new model runs. Planning also rolls out in a reasoned state space, whereas replay targets are detached backbone states; replay can mix stale latent coordinate systems as the backbone changes. Those are trainability/alignment problems, not evidence of superintelligence.

**New measured development result:** three seeds, actual V2 with fixed public action feedback, online world-scoped updates and replay. Hybrid all-path action accuracy averaged34.90%; disabling search gave43.49%. The surrounding-system Transformer averaged31.12% with search and 41.02% without it. The hybrid had62,366 parameters versus45,926, and active compute was not matched. A hand-coded cue decoder can solve this intentionally simple task, so neither neural score is a realistic multimodal benchmark. New development worlds deliberately had no past memory/fast records, so their no-memory/no-fast ablations cannot demonstrate learned transfer from those mechanisms.

**Concrete proposed repair:** `si_factual_search_loss.py` supervises value from completed observed reward sequences, trains dynamics/reward in the logged planning-state coordinates, and trains the verifier at the state where inference queries it. Four derivative/integrity tests pass. This is a tested training-graph proposal, **not a trained successful SI successor**. Caller provenance remains an assertion, not an independently authenticated environment receipt.

**Persistence limitation:** the first SI pilot saved neural `state_dict` checkpoints and detailed outputs, but did not export its external memory/fast-state/replay Python objects. These are weight-only checkpoints, not full persistent-agent snapshots. The recorded evaluation on a new empty-memory development world is reproducible in scope; a complete lifetime-learning reproduction needs explicit state/replay export and model-version-aware replay. No historical attempt is rerun to conceal this limitation.

**Decision:** pause expensive full-system scaling until value learning, rollout/replay coordinates, persistent state, factual environment boundaries and same-system compute controls work. Continue targeted repairs and one independently chosen action-feedback environment. The complete SI architecture remains scientifically unvalidated, rather than proved impossible by proxy tests.

## What would change these decisions?

Before increasing a candidate toward the user's ceiling: valid positive/negative controls, repeated independent seeds, a meaningful advantage over a strong alternative, matched supervision/data and measured full training/inference cost. Preserve all failed attempts and unused/consumed evaluation identities. Use three sizes that actually stay under100M and multiple token horizons with declared learning-rate schedules; fit only within the measured range. A100/T4/L4 results are hardware-specific and cannot be interchanged without remeasurement. TPU requires a separate XLA implementation; its availability is not an allocation or evidence of portability.

Modern comparison is necessary because recurrence, memory updates and attention hybrids already have established alternatives: [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464), [Griffin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19427), [RWKV-7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14456). Their relevance is a mechanism/comparison inference; this work did not train those baselines or reproduce their published scores.

Prioritize **Conv7 durability, CPW local-mixer scaling and CHM independent generalization**; pursue **RLT robustness and SI training-graph repair** cheaply; require **PGW learnability/control repair** before more scale. No result here justifies a broad breakthrough label. No small invalid-control pilot justifies abandoning an entire family.
