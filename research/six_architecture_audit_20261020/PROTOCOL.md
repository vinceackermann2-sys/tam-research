## Execution contract

Before optimization: save source digests, recipe, parameter count, seed, data identity, device and a create-once attempt file. Token guard checks the next complete batch before it runs and never rounds up through a ceiling. Save terminal errors and partial exposure counts. No automatic retry, rescue seed, silent resume or result overwrite. Retain every result.

The first batch may diagnose bugs, capacity or learnability. Passing it only prioritizes a later preregistered equal-data/equal-compute study; it cannot establish a breakthrough. A general research stop requires valid positive controls and repeated fair negative results at a finite budget. Broad claims additionally need strong recurrent/hybrid controls, independent seeds/tasks and useful inference cost.
